About Nature's Views

Photography • Tourism • Visual Storytelling

Discover the Beauty of Africa

Nature's Views is a photography and visual storytelling platform dedicated to showcasing the beauty, culture, history and natural resources of Sierra Leone and Africa.

Through photography and storytelling, we aim to promote tourism, preserve memories and introduce the world to amazing destinations across Sierra Leone.

Our vision is to grow beyond Sierra Leone and tell Africa's story through powerful images and meaningful experiences.

Meet the Founder

The person behind Nature's Views

Eugene Raymond Campbell

Founder & C.E.O — Nature's Views

Eugene Raymond Campbell is the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of Nature's Views, a photography, tourism and visual storytelling platform focused on Sierra Leone and Africa.

His passion is to capture the beauty of nature, historical places, communities and cultural experiences while promoting Sierra Leone as a destination worth discovering.

Through Nature's Views, Eugene aims to use photography and storytelling to connect people with Africa and inspire more people to explore and appreciate its beauty.
