About Nature's Views

Photography • Visual Storytelling • Tourism Promotion

Our Story

Nature's Views is a photography, visual storytelling and tourism-promotion platform focused on Sierra Leone and Africa. We showcase natural resources, landscapes, historical places, culture, communities and destinations that deserve to be seen and appreciated.


Our vision is to help tell the real and beautiful stories of Africa through powerful images and meaningful visual experiences.

Our Mission

To capture the beauty of Sierra Leone, promote tourism, preserve visual memories and tell Africa's story through authentic photography and creative visual storytelling.

📸 Photography

Professional and destination-focused photography for people, places, events and tourism.

🌍 Tourism Promotion

Visual promotion of tourism destinations, natural attractions, culture and hidden beauty.

🎥 Visual Storytelling

Creative stories that connect audiences with communities, places and experiences.

Founder & CEO

The person behind Nature's Views

Eugene Raymond Campbell

Founder and CEO of Nature's Views

Eugene Raymond Campbell is the Founder and CEO of Nature's Views, a photography, visual storytelling and tourism-promotion platform focused on Sierra Leone and Africa.


Through Nature's Views, he aims to showcase the beauty, history, culture, communities and hidden destinations of Sierra Leone while contributing to a wider visual story of Africa.


The long-term vision is to grow Nature's Views beyond Sierra Leone and use photography to connect people around the world with authentic African places and stories.
